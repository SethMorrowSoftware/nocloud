Drop your television programs in this folder, one folder per show:

  TV/
    The Expanse/
      poster.jpg                    shown on the shows wall (optional)
      Season 1/
        The.Expanse.S01E01.mkv
        The.Expanse.S01E01.en.srt   subtitles, auto-detected
      Season 2/
        ...
    Fawlty Towers/                  season folders are optional -
      Fawlty.Towers.1x01.mp4        episodes straight in the show folder work too

Nice to know:
  * Episode numbers are read from the file name: S01E02, 1x02, or E02 all work
    (S01E01E02 / S01E01-E02 mark a double episode).
  * Season folders can be "Season 1", "Season 01", "S2", "Series 3", or "Specials".
  * Watched episodes are remembered per browser, and the player offers the next
    episode when one ends.

This file is ignored by the media center; feel free to delete it.
